✅ What happened

I asked Claude to split my herdr screen into 4. Claude ran the herdr pane split CLI itself, with no keyboard shortcuts, and the layout came out as four equal 109×36 panes.

herdr pane split w4:p1 --direction right --ratio 0.5
herdr pane split w4:p1 --direction down  --ratio 0.5
herdr pane split w4:p2 --direction down  --ratio 0.5
